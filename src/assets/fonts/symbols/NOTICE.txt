Terax Symbols is Symbols Nerd Font 3.5.1 (https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts),
split into one WOFF2 file per icon set and stripped of hinting by
scripts/symbols-font/build.py. Glyph outlines are unchanged.
